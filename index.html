About

My approach to orthodontics is grounded in thoughtful diagnosis, precise treatment planning, and an appreciation for the relationship between form and function.

Before dentistry, I studied architecture and worked in an architectural practice. That experience shaped the way I think about proportion, structure, spatial relationships, and design—principles that continue to influence the way I approach orthodontic care.

Advanced training in craniofacial and interdisciplinary orthodontics has further strengthened that perspective. Treating complex patients requires careful biomechanics, long-term planning, and close collaboration across specialties. Those same skills inform the comprehensive orthodontic care I provide every day.
